## Why this matters for SEO, AEO and GEO

Search engines and AI answer engines decide what a business is by matching its name, address, phone and website across many sources. Today those signals disagree and the official site says very little, so neither Google nor AI assistants have a confident picture of GSP.

1. Agree one canonical name, address, phone and website for each office.
2. Claim and fix the main listings: Google Business Profile (check whether one exists), BBB (add website, fix phone), Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, MapQuest/Yelp data sources.
3. Add Organization and LocalBusiness structured data on the new site that matches those details exactly.
4. Check whether GSP holds an FMC (OTI/NVOCC) license or customs broker license; if so, show it. These are strong trust and entity signals. A quick search did not confirm either way.
