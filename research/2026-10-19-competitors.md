# 2026-10-19 — Competitor research

**Method:** the team's partner shortlisted sites (design and industry). Claude read each homepage, the Clutch New Jersey shipping list, and ran searches for project cargo, Korean-American and Chicago-area forwarders. Stars are the team's rating of which sites are worth studying.

## The headline finding

**None of the shortlisted sites competes for GSP's actual customers.** They are good references for design and marketing, but they sell to different buyers:

| Site | What they really are | Who they sell to | Competes with GSP? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [LOKI 3PL](https://loki3pl.com/) ⭐⭐ | Asset-based 3PL: warehousing, e-commerce fulfillment, trucking. East Brunswick, NJ | E-commerce and retail brands | No: fulfillment, not international forwarding |
| [Shore to Shore Shipping](https://shipshoretoshore.com/) ⭐⭐ | USA–Caribbean forwarder, Union, NJ and Jamaica | Families, expats, small businesses shipping to Jamaica | No: consumer and Caribbean |
| [Tropical Shipping](https://www.tropical.com/) ⭐ | Ocean carrier for the Caribbean (Saltchuk group) | Shippers to the islands | No: a carrier GSP might book with |
| [Universal Relocations](https://www.universalrelocations.com/) | International household mover, USA–India–UAE | Households, expats, corporate moves | Barely |
| [NTS Logistics](https://ntslogistics.com/) | Trucking broker, Florida, heavy haul | Domestic shippers | No: domestic trucking |
| [Craters & Freighters NJ](https://www.cratersandfreightersnewjersey.com/) | Crating franchise, Middlesex, NJ (machinery, art) | Businesses needing crating | No: **a potential partner** |
| [Mercer Transportation](https://mercer-trans.com/) | Owner-operator trucking carrier, heavy haul | Domestic shippers | No: **a potential partner** for inland legs |

GSP's real competitors are B2B international forwarders: NY/NJ port forwarders (especially those doing project cargo), Chicago-area forwarders, Korean-American forwarders in the Fort Lee / Bergen County area, and the big digital forwarders (Flexport, Expeditors, DHL, C.H. Robinson) that dominate "best forwarders" lists. See [Next steps](#next-steps).

## LOKI 3PL ⭐⭐

**What works and is worth borrowing**
- **Separate quote buttons per service** ("Get Warehousing Quote", "Get Truckload / LTL Quote") instead of one generic form. GSP equivalent: Ocean, Air, Project Cargo, Customs.
- **A visible six-step process**, with a concrete promise (launch in 24–48 hours).
- **Language support banner** (Spanish and Mandarin). GSP can do the same with **Korean and Japanese**, which is real: Jason speaks both.
- **"How did you hear about us?" includes "ChatGPT / AI".** Copy this. It is the simplest way to measure whether AEO/GEO work brings leads.
- Every location listed with address and map; named testimonials with job titles.

**Weaknesses**
- Inconsistent facts (11 vs. 13 locations; a Fort Worth address that maps to Roanoke) and duplicated template blocks.
- Big-brand logos (Home Depot, Walmart, Amazon) under "Trusted by our partners" without saying what the relationship is.
- No licenses or certifications shown.
- WordPress + Elementor.
- Its #1 spot on Clutch's New Jersey list is a **sponsored placement with one review**.

## Shore to Shore Shipping ⭐⭐

**What works**
- **A niche content hub:** guides on shipping to Jamaica from New Jersey, container sizes, barrel shipping. This is exactly the model for GSP: guides on project cargo, heavy lift, and its main trade lanes.
- A clear experience stat ("37+ years"), FAQ section on the homepage, WhatsApp as a contact channel, and a warm "treat every customer like family" voice.
- Built recently on Next.js with WordPress as the content backend: a modern headless setup like the one planned for phase 2.

**Weaknesses:** no FMC license, no reviews, no named testimonials, unsupported claims ("countless customers").

## Others: one lesson each

- **Tropical Shipping:** a customer portal to book, pay and track, with how-to videos in three languages. Reference for the phase 4 client portal.
- **Universal Relocations:** the best trust-signal stack in the set: FMC OTI license, ISO 9001, industry memberships, Trustpilot and Google ratings, legal pages including its FMC tariff. Shows what a forwarder can display.
- **NTS Logistics:** BBB A+, Inc. 5000, industry rankings, Google reviews, a knowledge base and glossary.
- **Craters & Freighters NJ:** ISPM-15 certified export crating for machinery. A natural referral partner for project cargo.
- **Mercer Transportation:** flatbed and heavy-haul trucking across North America. A possible partner for inland legs of project cargo.

## Clutch (New Jersey shipping companies)

- About 50 NJ companies listed; **almost none have reviews**. LOKI 3PL is first through sponsorship with a single review; Clutch says it may earn fees for some placements but ranks mainly on verified reviews.
- **Opportunity:** a free Clutch profile for GSP plus 3–5 verified client reviews would put it near the top of a list where nobody has reviews, and adds a backlink.

## Lists that AI answer engines read

Searches for "best freight forwarders in Chicago" return listicles written by forwarders themselves (for example ExFreight, which ranks itself first). Their criteria include an in-house customs broker license and real local presence. AI answer engines draw heavily on these lists, so being included in them, and publishing GSP's own credible comparison content, is a GEO tactic.

## Industry directories found

- **Breakbulk** event exhibitor lists include NJ project cargo players (for example Harbor Freight Transport Corp., Newark, project cargo since 1947; Palco Transportation, NJ heavy haul). Exhibiting or joining is a strong niche signal.
- **forwarder.kr** lists Korean forwarders, including U.S.-based ones: a possible Korean-language listing.

## What this means for GSP

1. **Position on what the others do not have:** a founder with 20+ years, project cargo and heavy lift, and Korean/Japanese-language service. No site in this set combines those.
2. **Show credentials.** Almost every competitor leaves licenses off its site. GSP showing an FMC license number, customs brokerage, and memberships would stand out immediately.
3. **Build a content hub** around project cargo and trade lanes, following Shore to Shore's model.
4. **Measure AI referrals** with a "How did you hear about us? → ChatGPT/AI" option.
5. **Use partners, not just competitors:** crating, heavy-haul trucking and port operators are referral sources and backlinks.
