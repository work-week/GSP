# 2026-10-19 — Trust signals and backlinks

**Method:** review of the listings found in [online presence](2026-10-19-online-presence.md), plus general knowledge of how freight forwarders prove credibility. Licenses still need confirming with the client.

## What can go on the website as a trust badge

The rule: show only what is true, current and checkable. A badge a buyer can verify builds trust; a badge that turns out to be a paid listing loses it. Use a third party's logo only with its permission or under its published badge rules.

| Signal | Holds water? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FMC license (OTI: NVOCC or ocean freight forwarder)** | Strong | Public and searchable on the Federal Maritime Commission's site. Required in the U.S. to act as an ocean forwarder or NVOCC. Show the license number. Confirm whether GSP holds one. |
| **U.S. customs broker license** | Strong | GSP says it began as a customs broker. If licensed, show it. |
| **IATA / air cargo accreditation** | Strong if held | Relevant because they quote air freight. |
| **C-TPAT** | Strong if held | A CBP supply-chain security program; respected by importers. |
| **Forwarder networks** (for example WCA, other agent networks) | Good | Peers and shippers recognize them; GSP mentions a worldwide agent network. Ask which networks. |
| **Trade associations** (for example NCBFAA, regional forwarder associations) | Good | Membership shows standing in the industry. |
| **Local chambers of commerce** (Fort Lee, Schaumburg) | Moderate | Local trust plus a backlink. |
| **"Since 2011" / "20+ years"** | Good | Already true; BBB confirms the 2011 start date. |
| **BBB** | Weak today | GSP is "not rated" and not accredited. Do not show unless accredited. |
| **Freightnet "Premium Member"** | Weak | A paid directory membership, not a verification. Keep the listing for the backlink; do not present it as "verified". |
