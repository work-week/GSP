# Global Shipping Partners (GSP)

Knowledge base for our work with Global Shipping Partners: a freight forwarder founded in 2011, headquartered in Fort Lee, NJ, with an office in Schaumburg, IL, run by founder Jason P. Kwon.

**The short version:** GSP has 15 years of real operating history (and its founder 20+ years in the industry) but almost no visible proof of it online. The website is a dated WordPress site that attracts spam, the brand is barely findable on Google, and the contractor-built staging redesign did not win the client over. We are rebuilding the site and brand presence, then growing it into lead generation and, later, software.
