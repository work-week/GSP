# Scope: first phase (about 2 months)

Agreed direction as of 2026-10-19. Confirm details with the client before treating this as a contract.

## Goals the client cares about

- **Reach:** be found on Google and cited by AI answer engines.
- **Less spam:** stop the ~5 junk messages a day.
- **A site that represents GSP:** what the staging redesign failed to do.

## 1. Webflow website

- Start on a Webflow basic site plan for the core pages; upgrade to a CMS plan when the blog needs it.
- Core pages (draft list, to confirm): Home, About (story, Jason, offices), Services (one page per service: freight forwarding, air, ocean FCL, heavy lift and project cargo, customs and documentation, consulting), Industries, Request a Quote, Contact.
- Forms with spam protection; keep the Rate Inquiry fields.
- Carry over or redirect every old URL that matters (`/about/`, `/request-a-quote/`, `/contact-2-2/`, `/services`, `/freight-forwarding`, the `http` and `www` homepage variants, and so on; Semrush shows `/services` and `/freight-forwarding` indexed, 2026-10-19) so nothing that ranks or is linked breaks.

## 2. SEO, AEO and GEO groundwork

- Technical basics: titles, descriptions, headings, alt text, sitemap, redirects, page speed, mobile zoom fixed.
- Structured data: Organization and LocalBusiness for each office, Service, FAQ where real.
- Entity consistency: one name, address and phone everywhere; fix listings (see [online presence](../research/2026-10-19-online-presence.md)).
- Content: at least one strong blog post to start, plus FAQ content written to be quoted by answer engines.
- Backlinks: a respectable starting set from industry directories, associations, partners and local business listings. No paid link schemes.
- An ongoing optimization commitment for SEO, AEO and GEO after launch.

## Out of scope for this phase

The custom SvelteKit build, newsletters and lead nurturing, and the CRM and client portal. Those are later phases in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).
