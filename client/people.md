# People

Relationship context for working with the client. Business information only.

## Jason P. Kwon: founder, President and CEO

Sources: the GSP site and profile PDF, his [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jason-kwon-95116963) (screenshot shared by the team 2026-10-19), and what he told the team.

**Background**
- Founded GSP in 2011; says he has been in the shipping industry for about 21 years. The site and profile say 20+ years in international sales and shipping.
- Before that: 5+ years in a logistics department with U.S. suppliers (food chain, technology equipment).
- B.A. International Business, North Central College, Naperville, IL (1997–2001), minors in Japanese and History of Ideas. College Scholar; senior thesis on the 1997–99 Korean economic crisis.
- Dale Carnegie course graduate, four-time award recipient.
- Speaks English, Korean and Japanese.
- Stated goal: open offices worldwide to give local customer service.

**LinkedIn:** Fort Lee, NJ; about 66 connections and 68 followers; no recent posts; the profile shows little beyond his title and education. His personal online presence is as thin as the company's.

**Working with him**
- He is skeptical of AI and has been clear about it. Respect that. In conversations with him, lead with outcomes (being found, leads, less spam, a site that represents GSP) rather than tools. Never misrepresent how the work is done if he asks.
- He is very open to AEO/GEO (being cited by AI search and answer engines) and wants the site optimized for it "down to the dot."
- He was unhappy with the contractor's staging redesign because it did not represent the brand. See [brand.md](brand.md).
- His pain points, in his words via the team: reach of the website, and spam (up to about 5 a day), which he links to WordPress and its plugins.
