## Services (from the profile PDF; the website lists only "Freight Forwarding")

- **Freight forwarding:** export documentation, consular requirements and legalization, banking services (letters of credit, drafts), intermodal bookings, warehousing, door-to-door cargo insurance, automated export declarations to U.S. Customs, domestic and foreign inland transport, dangerous goods, break bulk and project cargo.
- **Air and sea freight:** the quote form offers air and sea, including FCL container types (20', 40', 45', open top, flat rack, reefer). LCL is not mentioned.
- **Heavy lift and project cargo:** power generation plants, oil and mining developments, oversized and out-of-gauge cargo.
- **Consulting:** trade, transportation and logistics consulting, program development, training.
