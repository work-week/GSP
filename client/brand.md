# Brand

Last updated: 2026-10-19.

## Where it stands

GSP has run for 15 years mostly under the radar: steady work through referrals and an agent network, but almost nothing public to show for it. The brand has never been properly expressed online.

## Current messaging (live site)

- Tagline: "Simplifying Logistics with great service." Homepage headline: "Simplifying logistics with unrivaled service."
- Themes: customer service, attention to detail, innovation, integrity, urgency, experience.
- Values from the About page: honesty, urgency, experience, integrity.
- Weak points: generic claims with no proof (for example "100% customer satisfaction"), one testimonial, no case studies, no numbers, no named trade lanes, no credentials shown.

## Real strengths to build the brand on

These come from the company's own materials; confirm with the client before using them publicly.

- **Founder-led, 20+ years of experience**, with a personal touch clients mention by name.
- **Heavy lift and project cargo** for power, oil and mining: a specialist niche, not a commodity service.
- **A trilingual founder** (English, Korean, Japanese), likely relevant to trans-Pacific trade.
- **Two U.S. offices** in major logistics hubs (New York/New Jersey port area and Chicago).
- **Full-service scope:** documentation, letters of credit, insurance, hazmat, customs filings.
